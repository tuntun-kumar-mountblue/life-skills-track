# Learning Process

### Q - What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you explain a big concept in simple words as if you are teaching someone else.

### Q - In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea for me was **focused mode and diffuse mode**. I learned that taking a short break when we are stuck can help our brain find a solution. It showed me that breaks are also important for learning.

### Q - What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Active (Focused) Mode:** When we concentrate fully on one problem and try to solve it directly.

**Diffuse Mode:** When we relax or take a break and let our brain make connections in the background.

### Q - According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

i. Learn enough to self-correct  
ii. Deconstruct the skill
