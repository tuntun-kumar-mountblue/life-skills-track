# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Questions

### 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any **unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature**, such as:

* Unwanted touching or physical contact.
* Sexual comments, jokes, or inappropriate conversations.
* Unwanted sexual advances or repeated requests for dates or favours.
* Sharing sexually explicit messages, videos, or content.
* Inappropriate comments about someone's body or appearance.
* Creating an uncomfortable, or offensive workplace.

### 2. What would you do if you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or witness such behaviour, I would:

1. Take the incident seriously and not ignore it.
2. If possible, tell the person that their behaviour is inappropriate.
3. Note down the incident and keep any relevant evidence.
4. Report it to the appropriate person or **Internal Committee (IC)**.
5. Support the affected person and maintain confidentiality.
