### Q1. What is Deep Work?

Deep Work means giving my complete attention to an important and difficult task without distractions. It helps me learn difficult things faster and produce better-quality work.

### Q2. How to do Deep Work properly?

* I should choose one important task at a time.
* I should remove distractions like my phone and social media.
* I should set a fixed time for focused work.
* I should take proper short breaks.
* I should practice deep work regularly and gradually increase my focus time.

### Q3. How can I implement these principles in my daily life?

I can keep 1–2 hours daily for important work or study. During this time, I can keep my phone away and avoid social media and multitasking. I can also take short breaks and spend some time walking or sitting without digital distractions.

### Q4. What are the dangers of social media?

Excessive use of social media can waste my time and reduce my concentration. It can become addictive and make it difficult for me to focus on important tasks. It can also lead to unnecessary comparison and stress.

### Getting Started on Difficult Tasks

When I feel afraid, bored, or frustrated about a difficult task, I can start by working on it for just 30 seconds. After a short break, I can remind myself that I am capable of completing it. By repeating this process and gradually increasing the time, I can build confidence and reduce procrastination.
