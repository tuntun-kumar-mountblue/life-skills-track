# Listening and Active Communication

## Q1. Active Listening

1. Pay full attention to listen the speaker.
2. Maintain eye contact.
3. Do not interrupt.
4. Ask questions when something is not clear.
5. Repeat or summarize the main points to confirm understanding.

## Q2. Reflective Listening

1. Listen carefully to the speaker and understand the speaker's emotions.
2. Understand the speaker's main message.
3. Repeat the message in your own words.
4. Confirm whether your understanding is correct.

## Q3. Obstacles in Listening

1. Mobile phones and other distractions like noise etc.
2. Lack of concentration.
3. Any health related issue like Overthinking.
4. Making assumptions too early.
5. Emotional reactions or disagreement.

## Q4. How Can I Improve My Listening?

1. Avoid distractions while listening.
2. Focus completely on the speaker.
3. Do not interrupt.
4. Ask questions when needed.
5. Take notes during important discussions.

## Q5. When Do I Switch to Passive Communication?

1. When I want to avoid conflict.
2. When I am afraid of hurting someone.
3. When I hesitate to share my opinion.
4. When I find it difficult to say "no".
5. When I want to keep peace in a group.

## Q6. When Do I Switch to Aggressive Communication?

1. When I feel frustrated.
2. When someone repeatedly ignores me.
3. When someone blames me unfairly.
4. When I am under too much pressure.

## Q7. When Do I Switch to Passive-Aggressive Communication?

1. When I use sarcasm instead of speaking directly.
2. When I give taunts.
3. When I give someone silent treatment.
4. When I gossip instead of discussing the problem.
5. When I say "it's fine" even though I am unhappy.

## Q8. How Can I Make My Communication Assertive?

1. Speak clearly and directly.
2. Use "I" statements.
3. Respect other people's opinions.
4. Stay calm and control emotions.
5. Clearly express my needs while respecting others.
