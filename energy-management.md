# Energy Management

## Q1. Calm Quadrant

* Listening to music
* Walking
* Watching movies
* Talking to friends
* Playing games
* Meditation

## Q2. Stress Quadrant

* During deadlines
* Difficult coding problems
* Interview preparation
* Too much work
* Learning something new

## Q3. Excitement Quadrant

* Feeling happy and energetic
* Enjoying the work
* Feeling motivated
* Being curious
* Staying focused

## Q4. Sleep Is Your Superpower

* Sleep helps the brain work better.
* It improves memory.
* It improves focus.
* Good sleep keeps us healthy.
* Lack of sleep causes tiredness and poor concentration.
* Good sleep improves mood.

## Q5. How to Sleep Better

* Sleep and wake up at the same time.
* Avoid phone before bed.
* Exercise regularly.
* Avoid caffeine at night.
* Keep the room quiet and dark.
* Get enough sleep.

## Q6. Benefits of Exercise

* Improves brain function.
* Improves memory.
* Reduces stress.
* Improves mood.
* Improves focus.
* Gives more energy.
* Keeps the brain healthy.

## Q7. How to Exercise More

* Walk every day.
* Exercise for 20–30 minutes.
* Take stairs.
* Play a sport.
* Exercise with friends.
* Make exercise part of the daily routine.
