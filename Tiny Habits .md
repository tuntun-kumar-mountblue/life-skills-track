### Question 1: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

### Question 2: How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

**B = MAP** means:

* **M – Motivation:** Desire to do something.
* **A – Ability:** How easy it is to do.
* **P – Prompt:** A reminder or trigger.

We can make habits easier by keeping them simple and connecting them with an existing routine.

### Question 3: Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit?

Celebrating creates a positive feeling. It helps the brain remember the behavior and makes us more likely to repeat it.

### Question 6: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

Make a habit easier by reducing the steps needed to do it. Keep the required things ready and start with a small task.

### Question 7: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

Make a bad habit harder by adding obstacles. For example, keep your phone away and turn off unnecessary notifications.
