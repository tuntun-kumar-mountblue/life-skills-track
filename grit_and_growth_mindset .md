# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1 — Grit

Means having passion to keep working toward a long-term goal, even when things become difficult. because
Success does not depends only on talent but also on consistent effort and determination.

## Question 2 — Introduction to Growth Mindset

A growth mindset is the belief that our abilities and intelligence can improve through learning, practice, and effort. Challenges and mistakes should be seen as opportunities to learn and grow.

## Question 3 — Internal Locus of Control

**Internal Locus of Control** means believing that our actions, choices, and efforts have a major influence on the results we achieve.

**Key point:** Focus on what you can control instead of blaming external factors. Take responsibility for your actions and work on things that are within your control.

## Question 4 — How to Build a Growth Mindset

- Learn from mistakes.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on effort and progress.
- Keep learning new things.
- Be persistent when facing difficulties.
- Learn from the success of others.
- Take risks and step outside your comfort zone.
- Believe that abilities can improve with practice.

## Question 5 — My Ideas to Take Action and Build Growth Mindset

- I will treat mistakes as learning opportunities instead of failures.
- I will ask for feedback from my seniors and teammates.
- When I face a difficult problem, I will try different approaches before giving up.
- I will focus on improving my skills rather than comparing myself with others.
- I will maintain consistency and track my progress.
- I will take responsibility for my work and focus on solutions rather than excuses.
- I will challenge myself to work on tasks that are outside my comfort zone.
- I will learn from experienced developers and apply their feedback.
